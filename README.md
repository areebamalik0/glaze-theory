# Glaze Theory

I made this landing page for a pretend ceramics studio to practice building something that feels designed, not templated. The studio, the pots and the prices are all invented.

Live version: https://areebamalik0.github.io/glaze-theory/

The part I like most is the big "Made by hand" title on the first screen. It's an outline until your cursor goes over it, and then the colour shows through, like a glaze coming out of the kiln. On a phone it moves by itself, since there's no cursor.

The pots in the product cards are drawn with CSS only. There are no images anywhere on the page. The cards tilt a little when you hover over them, the sections fade in as you scroll, and the menu turns into a full-screen one on small screens.

The contact form checks that the fields are filled in and the email looks right, but it doesn't send anything. It's just for show.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks. To run it, download the files and open index.html in your browser.
